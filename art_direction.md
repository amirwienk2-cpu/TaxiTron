# Art Direction Document: Zombie City (Mobile 2D)

## Visual Style & Render Technique
* **Style:** Macabre, high-quality 2D comic look.
* **Shading:** Light cel-shaded finish with subtle grain/texture.
* **Composition:** Portrait orientation, strictly top-down (birds-eye view).
* **Display Target:** Optimized for small mobile screens (Telegram displays) using large, bold shapes and strong edge contrast.

## Color Palette & Lighting
* **Palette:** Taxi Yellow, Asphalt Black, Blood Red, Toxic Green, Warm Neon-Orange.
* **Lighting:** Dramatic night lighting featuring high-contrast yellow headlights, warm orange ambient neon glows, and sharp red taillight highlights.

## Core Visual Elements & Sprites
* **Key Motifs:** Blood-splattered yellow taxi, sharply defined readable zombie silhouettes, cracked asphalt textures.
* **Sprite Requirements:** Clean top-down assets, fully isolated/transparent backgrounds, no embedded text, no borders or frames.
